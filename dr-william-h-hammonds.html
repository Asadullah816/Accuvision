Dr. William H. Hammonds

Board Certified Ophthalmologist

REFRACTIVE SURGEON & CORNEAL SPECIALIST

Over 20 Years of LASIK Excellence

Dr. William H. Hammonds is a Board Certified Ophthalmologist and Corneal Specialist who has been performing LASIK for over 20 years. In addition to completing his medical degree, Dr. Hammonds also completed a year in specialized training that was devoted specifically to corneal disease and refractive surgery (LASIK) at Bascom Palmer Eye Institute.

He has performed over 30,000 LASIK, cataract, and corneal surgeries. Prior to joining Accuvision LASIK, Dr. Hammonds performed refractive surgery at TLC Laser Eye Centers in Charlotte. Dr. Hammonds is the team eye physician and sole LASIK provider for the Carolina Panthers as well as the Charlotte Hornets.

EDUCATION / TRAINING

Dr. Hammonds received his undergraduate degree from the University of Notre Dame and his medical degree from the Medical College of Georgia. He completed his ophthalmology residency at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. Following his residency, he completed his fellowship in Cornea, External Disease and Refractive Surgery at Bascom Palmer, which is nationally ranked as the #1 eye care center in the United States by US News and World Report.

MEMBERSHIPS / AFFILIATIONS

Dr. Hammonds is a Board Certified Ophthalmologist and an active member of the American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO) and the American Society of Cataract and Refractive Surgery (ASCRS).
