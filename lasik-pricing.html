Transparent, Affordable LASIK Pricing

No down payment prior to surgery. No taxes. No hidden fees!

LASIK is a customized procedure, and costs can vary. At Accuvision LASIK, our expert team ensures transparent pricing. Your LASIK consultation is always complimentary, and there is no down payment required prior to your LASIK procedure.

Full fees are collected on your procedure day. We believe everyone deserves crystal-clear eyesight without financial stress or surprise medical bills.

Finance & Payment Options

We offer No Interest and Extended Payment Plans to make LASIK accessible. Additionally, you can use Flex Plans or alternative health savings accounts to cover costs.

CareCredit 0% Financing

CareCredit with zero percent interest plans up to 18 months allows you to pay comfortably over time with zero interest charges.

Extended Payment Plans

Extended payment options up to 48 months offering budget-friendly monthly payments that fit your family's financial goals.

Flexible Payment Methods

Pay in full with check or credit card. Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express are proudly accepted.

FSA & HSA Eligible

Use pre-tax dollars from your Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or Health Savings Account (HSA) for substantial tax savings on your procedure.

Contact us today to schedule your free LASIK consultation and discuss your financing options!
