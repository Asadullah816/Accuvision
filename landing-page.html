The Technology

All-Laser LASIK.

Accuvision LASIK uses the WaveLight® Refractive Suite — the gold standard in laser vision correction. This advanced system pairs two precise lasers for a fully blade-free, all-laser LASIK procedure.

  • WaveLight® FS200 Femtosecond Laser
  • WaveLight® EX500 Excimer Laser
  • All-Laser & Blade-Free
  • Wavefront-Guided Precision
⚡
WaveLight® FS200 Femtosecond Laser Creates the corneal flap with ultra-precise, blade-free technology for maximum safety and consistency.
🎯
WaveLight® EX500 Excimer Laser Precisely reshapes the cornea to correct your vision — fast, accurate, and customized to your eyes.
🔬
Wavefront-Guided Customization Your treatment is tailored to the unique measurements of your eyes for the best possible visual outcome.

Common Questions

Yes! Your LASIK consultation at Accuvision LASIK is completely complimentary and includes a comprehensive eye exam, diagnostic testing, and plenty of time to get your questions answered — with absolutely no obligation to proceed with LASIK.
During your complimentary consultation, Dr. Rauch will evaluate your eye health, prescription stability, and corneal measurements to determine whether LASIK or PRK may be right for you. She'll review your results in detail and explain all your options clearly.
LASIK typically takes about 20 minutes for both eyes, with the laser treatment lasting just seconds per eye. Plan to spend about two hours at the office to allow time for preparation, the procedure itself, and a review of your aftercare instructions.
Most patients report little to no discomfort during LASIK. Numbing eye drops are applied before the procedure, so you may feel mild pressure but no pain. After the procedure, some temporary dryness or light sensitivity is normal as your eyes heal.
Most patients notice dramatically improved vision within 24 hours of their LASIK procedure and can return to work and most normal activities the very next day. Dr. Hammonds will provide detailed aftercare instructions and schedule follow-up visits to monitor your healing.
