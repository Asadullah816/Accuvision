Are You a Candidate for LASIK Surgery?

Take the Quiz to Find Out! Curious if LASIK is right for you? Every eye is different, and while LASIK can provide clear vision for many, it’s important to know if you’re an ideal candidate. Take our quick and easy quiz to assess your eligibility for LASIK surgery.

Question 1: What is your age?

Age helps us assess refractive stability and corneal maturation.

Question 2: What vision issues do you experience?

Select what your current glasses or contacts are prescribed for:

Question 3: Has your eye prescription remained stable for at least 1 year?

Stable prescriptions ensure lasting surgical results without premature drift.

Question 4: Are you currently pregnant or nursing?

Hormonal fluctuations during pregnancy can temporarily alter corneal curvature.

Question 5: Do you have a history of eye diseases (cataracts, glaucoma, keratoconus)?

Healthy corneal and retinal structures are required for optimal refractive outcomes.
